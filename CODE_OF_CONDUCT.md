# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want 900API to be a useful and welcoming project for contributors and users across countries, languages, income levels, abilities, and experience levels.

Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other communication conducted on behalf of the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique code and decisions without attacking people.
- Make room for contributors who communicate in a second language or are new to open source.
- Share enough context for others to understand a problem.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private endpoints, customer data, or personal information.
- Accept a maintainer decision on scope after concerns have been heard and explained.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized language
- Personal insults, intimidation, or sustained disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private data, or security details
- Spam, manipulation, or unrelated promotion

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Enforcement should be proportional, documented privately when practical, and focused on keeping the project safe and workable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@900labs.com`. Include links and relevant context, but do not collect or forward unrelated personal information. Reports will be reviewed by project maintainers and handled with reasonable confidentiality.

This code applies to maintainers as well as contributors.
